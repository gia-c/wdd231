About Me

My name is Gisela. I have a degree in Business Administration and I am currently studying Software Development. After several years working in a Chinese Tech company, I decided to pursue a career in technology to combine my passion for problem-solving with continuous learning.

Throughout my career, I have worked in hospitality, retail, and technology industries. Today, I work for a global athleisure brand that combines technology and fashion. Living in Argentina and the United States, along with traveling to other countries, has strengthened my adaptability and appreciation for different cultures.

I speak English, Spanish, and French, and I enjoy learning new technologies, building practical solutions, and expanding my skills as I continue my journey in software development.
